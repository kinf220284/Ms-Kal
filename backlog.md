# The Backlog — everything, out of your head 🧠➡️📄

**Read this rule first, every time:** you do not read the whole list. You
scan to the top unchecked item in the section you're working, do *that one
thing*, cross it off, and close the file. The pile is heavy only when you hold
all of it at once. On paper it's just a queue.

## 🔵 Admin batch — the "mental weight" (each one is small!)

These feel huge because they've been renting space in your head *together*.
Individually they're a phone call or a short errand. **Batch them:** pick ONE
"admin block" (60–90 min) and knock out as many calls as you can back-to-back.
Calls first (business hours), errands second.

Ordered by *do-this-first* (time-sensitive / money-owed at the top):

- [ ] **Call Deloitte** about the bonus you're eligible for (before you quit).
  *Time-sensitive — money owed to you. Do this one first.*
  - Have ready: employee ID, last day, who mentioned the eligibility.
- [ ] **Call Vanguard** about the 401k now that you've left Deloitte
  (roll over / options). *Money — do soon, no rush to the minute.*
- [ ] **Pay the overdue Mount Sinai fee.** *Overdue = accruing / dinging you.*
  - Online portal first; call billing if the portal won't cooperate.
- [ ] **Deposit the mailed checks** into your bank account.
  - Mobile deposit in the banking app is usually 2 minutes — try that before
    driving anywhere.
- [ ] **Call to cancel the local gym membership.** *Stops the recurring charge.*
- [ ] **Return the Amazon order to Whole Foods.** *Errand — pair with a walk
    or a climbing-gym trip so it doubles as leaving the house + light.*

> When these are done, notice how much lighter your head is. That feeling is
> the whole point — the tasks were never the weight, carrying them was.

### How to work this list
1. Weight in your chest? → **Admin batch.** Do the top unchecked call.
2. Deep-work block? → Pick **one** 🟣 project, do its **next action** only.
3. New worry pops up? → Write it in 🟢 immediately so it stops circling.
4. Crossed something off? → Actually cross it off. Let yourself feel it.
