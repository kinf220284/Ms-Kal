# This week: reclaim the mornings ☀️

**Week of Mon Jul 6 – Sun Jul 12, 2026**

## The one goal

> **Wake before 9:00 by Sunday.**

Not "fix my whole life." Not "clear the backlog." Just move the wake time.
Everything else this week is a bonus. If you only do this, the week is a win.

## The wake ramp (gentle — no cold turkey)

You're going from ~11:30/12 → before 9 by shifting ~30 min every couple days.
The **wake time** is the target. The bedtime is what your body does on its own
once it's getting morning light — don't force it, just don't fight sleep when
it comes.

## The non-negotiables (do these even on a bad day)

Every morning, in this order, before phone/email/work:
1. ☐ **Open ALL the curtains.** Room stays bright all day. This is the keystone.
2. ☐ **Light in your eyes for 10 min** — step outside, balcony, or sit at the
   brightest window. Coffee out there if you want.
3. ☐ **Feet on the floor + a glass of water.** You're up. That's the win.

That's it. If the rest of the day falls apart, you still won the morning.

## Daily checklist

Copy [`daily/TEMPLATE.md`](daily/TEMPLATE.md) each morning. Minimum viable day:
- ☐ Hit (or got close to) the wake target
- ☐ Curtains + light
- ☐ Moved your body once (even a 10-min walk counts — see `rhythm.md`)
- ☐ Did **one** thing off [`backlog.md`](backlog.md)
- ☐ Wind-down started ~1 hr before "in bed by"

## The "leave the house" rule

The spiral says *"it's 4pm, too late to go out."* New rule: **there is no time
that's too late to open the door.** A 15-minute walk at 6pm still counts, still
gives light, still breaks the cave. Late is not a reason to skip. Ever.
